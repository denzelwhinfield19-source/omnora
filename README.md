
OMNORA

The World at Your Order

Our Mission

OMNORA is a universal purchasing network designed to connect consumers, businesses, retailers, wholesalers, and suppliers through one convenient digital platform.

Our goal is to make discovering, comparing, sourcing, and purchasing products easier across local and international markets.

Core Features

- Universal product search
- Product comparison across participating sellers
- Customer accounts
- Business and supplier registration
- Product listings and inventory management
- Shopping cart and order management
- Business sourcing and quotation requests
- Seller dashboards
- Administrator dashboard
- Transparent pricing and commissions
- Order tracking and customer support
- International expansion capabilities

Business Model

OMNORA combines:

1. A marketplace where independent suppliers sell products.
2. Selected products purchased and owned by OMNORA for resale.

Potential revenue streams include:

- Marketplace commissions
- Product sales margins
- Business subscriptions
- Promoted listings
- Product sourcing services

Technology Plan

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Supabase
- PostgreSQL
- GitHub
- Vercel or another suitable hosting provider

Development Roadmap

Phase 1: Create the prototype.
Phase 2: Build the product catalogue and search.
Phase 3: Recruit suppliers.
Phase 4: Implement ordering and order management.
Phase 5: Add secure payment integrations.
Phase 6: Test real deliveries.
Phase 7: Expand into additional markets.

Security Principles

- Protect customer information.
- Verify participating suppliers.
- Enforce access permissions.
- Keep payment credentials secure.
- Maintain transaction records.
- Test security before launch.

Current Status

OMNORA is in the initial prototype development stage.

Global supplier coverage, payment integrations, and international fulfilment will be developed and verified progressively.

Long-Term Vision

Build a trusted global purchasing destination connecting people and businesses with products and suppliers around the world.
